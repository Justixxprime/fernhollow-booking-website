Fernhollow Rewards

Every stay earns you
the next one, a little cheaper.

One points program across the whole collection. No annual fee, no tiers you have to chase, just real credit for stays you were already going to book. This page reads your own confirmed bookings on this device, nothing is faked.

How it works

Earn 1 point per dollar

Every completed booking's total (nightly rate, add-ons, taxes and fees) earns points automatically, no sign-up step.

Climb through tiers

Sprout → Grove → Old Growth. Higher tiers unlock better perks, not just a badge.

Redeem for real discounts

1,000 points = $10 off your next stay. There's no minimum booking length or blackout dates.

Tiers & perks

Tier Stays booked Perk
🌱 Sprout 0-1 Points on every stay, from your first booking.
🌿 Grove 2-4 Free early check-in add-on on every booking.
🌲 Old Growth 5+ 10% off every stay automatically, plus a free firewood bundle.

Refer a friend

Share your code. When a friend enters it before their first booking, you both get a bonus. Demo note: since this is a portfolio project with no shared server, referral credit only works within one browser, open a private/incognito window to try entering your own code as a "friend" would.
