Before you book

A few things people usually ask.

How does cancellation work?

Every stay offers free cancellation up to 5 days before check-in for a full refund. Inside that window, the first night plus the service fee become non-refundable.

Can I bring a pet?

Some stays are pet-friendly and some aren't, check the policies section on each stay's detail page, or filter the browse page by "Pet friendly".

Is there wifi?

Yes, every stay has wifi, though speeds vary by location, and a few of our more remote cabins run on satellite internet, which is noted on the listing if so.
